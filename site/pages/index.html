Research process

From idea to ownership.

A consistent, repeatable sequence applied to every opportunity we consider — designed so that decisions are made on evidence rather than sentiment.

  1. 01

    Source

    Identify opportunities through proprietary research, industry relationships, and long-term market themes.

  2. 02

    Research

    Study the underlying business, management team, competitive environment, and industry structure.

  3. 03

    Underwrite

    Evaluate potential returns, downside scenarios, liquidity, and key risks.

  4. 04

    Invest

    Deploy capital selectively when conviction and opportunity are aligned.

  5. 05

    Monitor

    Continuously review performance, risks, assumptions, and changing market conditions.
